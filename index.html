About this page

Hi there! Basically, what you see here is a pixel presentation of 80s he-man action figure series.

I'm something of a collector of these old toys and one day I had an idea to draw a retro pixel art image of one. A small experiment in simplification: how to present a figure in 32x32 pixels. Then I did another, and eventually some more. (If you're into this kind of nonsense, I started with the Battle Beasts series, this MotU catalog is the second one, and yes, I am working on third one).
